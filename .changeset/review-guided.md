New: a guided review. Press `w` (or click **guide** in the panel's header) and the conversation's model presents the changes as a short slide deck, in a dialog: organized by idea rather than by file, each slide explaining what the code does and what to look at, with the changed lines it's about. While it writes, the dialog streams what the model is doing, then the deck opens there. `←` / `→` move between slides; closing keeps the guide, and `w` opens it again at the same slide. Guides and questions share one helper session per reviewed session, so a question can ask about a slide.
