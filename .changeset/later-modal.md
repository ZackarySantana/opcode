`/later` is now a dialog in the format of `/feed` and `/ghpr`, with Later, History, and Settings tabs, search, and the same keys; `c` completes or restores an item, and `enter` closes the dialog and pastes the item into the prompt. The sidebar is quieter, lists 5 items by default (3 to 20 in Settings), and points to `/later` for the rest. When Later's server side is loaded, the agent can list, add, and complete Later items, and delete one after you approve it.
