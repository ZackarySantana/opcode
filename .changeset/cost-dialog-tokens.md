`/cost` is now a dialog, like `/feed` and `/ghpr`, with one search box and an in-dialog date range picker (`d`). Opening a longest turn closes the dialog and opens its session. Amounts show tokens beside cost; a view with no recorded cost, as on a subscription, hides cost and shows only tokens, and breakdowns rank cost ties by tokens.
