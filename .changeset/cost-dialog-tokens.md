`/cost` is now a dialog, like `/feed` and `/ghpr`: Cost and Time tabs, one search box that narrows the breakdowns and longest turns, and in-dialog pickers for the date, project, and model filters (`d`, `p`, `m`), which both tabs share. Opening a longest turn closes the dialog and opens its session. Amounts show tokens beside cost; a view with no recorded cost, as on a subscription, hides cost and shows only tokens, and breakdowns rank cost ties by tokens.
