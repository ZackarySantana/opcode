Add Review: `/review` opens the agent's changes in a panel beside the conversation, updating as it edits. Comment on a line or range, then send the drafts with `S` (or `esc`, which also closes the panel); they reach the running agent at its next step, with the file and lines attached. The agent replies to each with `review_reply`, shown under your comment and in the list of every comment (`C`), which marks replies that are new, and a comment ending in `??` asks a question instead of a change. `u` and `d` find the usages and the definition of the name under the cursor, by the language server OpenCode is set up with or a search by name, and flag uses the change may have missed; open one to comment on it, even outside the diff. `/` searches the paths and lines of the diff. The options menu (`o`) picks what to review (this turn, the whole session, uncommitted changes, or the branch against its base) and lists files as a folder tree or full paths; files stay marked viewed until their diff changes.
