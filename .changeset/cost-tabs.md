`/cost` has Overview, Models, Projects, Daily, and Turns tabs instead of panels stacked in one view. The Overview holds every total; each breakdown row shows its usage and its time together, and lists every model (now split by variant, as on the time side), project, and date in the range rather than the top eight or newest 14. ↑/↓ select rows one at a time and scroll to keep the selection in view; enter on a turn still opens its session. The date range (`d`) is the only filter: the project and model pickers and `x` reset are gone, along with the server's project/model filter parameters and picker options.
