Pull requests in the sidebar and `/ghpr` are listed newest first by the time they show, not by GitHub's last update: review requests by when your review, or your team's, was requested, and your own pull requests and ones you've reviewed by when they opened. Requests with no known request time, such as ones for a hidden team, come after the rest.
